Age 16

I wanted to be a doctor.

I imagined dramatic surgeries and saving lives. I was obssesed with Grey's Anatomy.

I imagined medicine as something exciting, purposeful, and heroic.

I did not imagine exhaustion becoming part of the job description.

I joined a first responder program to see what the future could look like.

I realized that healing people involved so much more than the parts shown on tv.

At some point, caring for people stopped feeling imaginary.

I realied I wanted to help people, but not through medicine.
