Age 12

I wanted to be a baker.

I imagined frosting cupcakes. I did not imagine waking up before sunrise everyday.

I imagined opening a bakery where people would smile after tasing my food.

I did not know that passion could still become labor.

I imagined spending the rest of my life doing something I loved

I did not know that loving something and surviving off of it were two different things.

At some point, I realized I wanted to create comfort more than desserts.

Maybe being appreciated felt better than being admired.
