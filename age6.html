Age 6

I wanted to be a princess.

I imagined being loved by everyone. I did not imagine being watched by everyone.

I wanted to wear those sparkly gowns, glass heels, and hair that never moved out of place.

I did not know that appearances could become a full-time responsibility.

I imagined dancing in grand ballrooms and meeting my prince charming.

I did not imagine cameras following every relationship I would ever have.

At some point, I realized I did not want a life that belonged to everyone else.

I think what I really wanted was to be noticed.
