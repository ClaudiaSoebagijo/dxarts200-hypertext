Age 18

I wanted to be a ui/ux designer.

I imagined turning creativity into a career people would actually take seriously.

I imagined designing apps and websites that felt beautiful and effortless to use

I did not realize how much invisible work goes into making something feel simple.

I spent hours staring at interfaces and thinking about what I would change.

I realized that every user expriences the same screen differently.

At some point, I became more interested in solving the problem than designing solution.

I started caring more about systems than surfaces.
