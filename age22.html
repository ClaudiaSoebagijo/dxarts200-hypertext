Age 22

I want to be a product manager.

I imagined finally finding a role where empathy, creativity, and technology could exist together.

I imagined helping build products that people genuinely needed.

I know that every decisions made will involve tradeoffs between users, business needs, and reality.

I liked translating confusion into something people could actually understand and use.

I know that meaningful products are built through uncertainty, compromise and iteration.

This is the future I chose for myself.

All the others still exist somewhere as alternate versions of me.
